SETUP GUIDE

Everything to get your gates ready. Stuck on any step? Email contact@equalsplit.org or call 805-367-6757 and we'll walk you through it.

You Do Not Need the App

Your gates time runs entirely on their own. Everything on this page works straight out of the box, with no phone, no pairing, and no account. The companion app is optional and only adds saving and reviewing results over time.

WHAT'S IN THE BOX

  • Gate 1 — the gate with the display and buttons
  • Gate 2 — the second gate
  • 2 connectors (bases the gates sit into)
  • 6 poles

A USB-C charging cable is not included. Any standard USB-C cable and charger will work — most cables you already have around will fit.

1. CHARGE BOTH GATES

Before first use, charge each gate fully. Plug a standard USB-C cable into the USB-C port on each gate and connect it to any USB power source. Give both gates a full charge before heading out to the track.

2. ASSEMBLE THE STANDS

Slide the poles into the cylindrical holes on each connector, then seat a gate into the connector. Push the poles in firmly so everything is stable, but don't force them. Repeat for the second gate. Set each stand on level ground where it won't tip.

3. POWER ON

Press the power button on each gate to turn it on.

4. POSITION THE GATES

Each gate senses on its own, so there's nothing to align between them. Point the sensor toward where the runner will pass and set the gate down. Height is fixed — the poles seat into the connector and the gate stands at its working height, with nothing to adjust.

The one thing that matters: the athlete has to pass within about 1 metre of the gate. Outside that, the gate won't see them and won't trigger. Set each gate close to the running line, not off at the edge of the track.

Gate 1 is the start and Gate 2 is the finish, so place Gate 1 where you want timing to begin. The two gates stay in radio contact past 100 m, so put them as far apart as your drill needs. Keep the metre in front of each gate clear once you've armed a run, or the gate will read it as the athlete going by.

5. TIMING A RUN

Gate 1 is the gate with the screen and the two buttons. Everything is controlled from that gate. Facing it:

  • Button 1 is the button nearer the screen.
  • Button 2 is the button farther from the screen.

Turn both gates on, then pick a mode from the home screen.

Mode 1 — Gate-to-Gate Split

Press Button 1 to arm Mode 1. The gates are now waiting. The clock starts the moment something passes Gate 1 and stops the moment something passes Gate 2, and the split appears on the screen.

This is the mode for flys (10 m, 20 m, 30 m), 40-yard dashes, and any drill where you want a clean time between two points. Nothing else to press — arm it and run.

Mode 2 — Speed Profile

Press and hold Button 2. The clock starts the instant you release it, so release on your own "go." The gates then record two segments: your release to the Gate 1 break, and the Gate 1 break to the Gate 2 break.

That splits a sprint into its start and its top-end in a single run, which Mode 1 can't show you. Use it for block starts and standing starts where the acceleration phase matters as much as the finish.

Clearing a Result

When a result is on screen, press Button 1 to go back to the home screen. From there you can arm the next run.

6. IF SOMETHING ISN'T WORKING

The timer starts but never stops

The run began at Gate 1 but Gate 2 never registered a break. Check that Gate 2 is powered on and charged, that it's aimed across the lane at the same height as Gate 1, and that the athlete actually passed through the gap rather than around the outside of it. If Gate 2 is on and aimed correctly and still doesn't stop the clock, email or call us — that's a fault, not a setup mistake, and we'll replace the gate.

The timer starts on its own

Something crossed Gate 1 while it was armed. Keep the lane clear between arming the gates and the start of the run.

Nothing happens when an athlete runs through

The gates were probably never armed. From the home screen, press Button 1 for Mode 1 or hold and release Button 2 for Mode 2 before each run. A result left on screen also has to be cleared with Button 1 first.

A gate won't turn on

Charge it over USB-C and try again. If a fully charged gate still won't power on, contact us — every unit carries a 90-day warranty and we repair or replace at no charge.

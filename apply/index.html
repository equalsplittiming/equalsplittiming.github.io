Financial Aid Program

APPLY FOR A FREE UNIT

Every unit sold funds a donated one. If your program can't afford commercial timing equipment, this is for you. No cost, no catch. We read every application.

Who it's for

High school teams, club programs, youth organizations, and individual athletes. If cost is the barrier, you qualify to apply.

What you get

A complete system: two timing gates, accurate to roughly ±5ms. USB-C rechargeable, 100m+ wireless range, no subscription, no laptop required.

What happens next

We review applications as they come in and reach out by email. If we can't get to you right away, you go on the list for the next batch.
